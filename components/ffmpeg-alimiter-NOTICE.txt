Modifications:
  The AVFilter and libavutil infrastructure was removed. The lookahead ring,
  attack tracking and release state were adapted to a persistent streaming
  API over interleaved float32 buffers. The component supports only mono and
  stereo and fixes the final-export parameters to limit=-1 dBFS, attack=5 ms,
  release=100 ms, level=false, latency=true and asc=false. FFmpeg-compatible
  latency compensation is exposed as attack_frames - 1.
